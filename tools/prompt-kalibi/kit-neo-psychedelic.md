# Neo-Psychedelic Tattoo-Flash T-Shirt Prompt Kiti

---

## 1. ANA PROMPT (kopyala-yapıştır)

A vintage screen-printed t-shirt illustration in neo-psychedelic tattoo-flash style. **[ANA KONU]** rendered as a perfectly symmetrical, mirrored composition on a central vertical axis, enclosed in a tall art-nouveau arched frame (tombstone arch) with a thin black keyline border. Bold, uniform-weight black contour linework; all shading built exclusively from hand-stippled dotwork and hatching — no soft gradients, no airbrush. Flat spot-color separations, strictly limited palette: pale mint-turquoise, mustard yellow, coral salmon-pink, deep midnight navy-black, on a cream off-white paper ground. Celestial motifs woven in: a rayed sun with a serene sleeping face, moon-phase spheres arcing along the frame, tiny four-point stars, curling smoke/liquid tendrils. Radiating triangular light beams from the center. Heavy display serif wordmark **"[MARKA]"** beneath, plus a small arched outline-sans tagline **"[SLOGAN]"** and "EST. [YIL]". Riso/silkscreen texture, subtle halftone grain, slight ink misregistration, aged paper fibers. Flat 2D, no perspective, no photorealism, poster-flat depth.

### Negatif prompt

photorealistic, 3D render, soft gradients, airbrush, drop shadows, glossy, asymmetry, cluttered background, watermark, blurry, more than 5 colors, gradient mesh

### Midjourney parametreleri

`--ar 4:5 --style raw --stylize 250`

Stylize'ı 400'ün üstüne çıkarma — dotwork'ü bulanıklaştırıyor.

## 3. STİLİ TUTAN 5 KRİTİK ÖBEK

Konuyu tamamen değiştirsen bile bunlar kalırsa çıktı hep aynı aileden görünür. Asla çıkarma:

1. **stippled dotwork shading, no gradients** — asıl imzayı bu verir
2. **flat spot-color separations, limited palette** — dijital "AI parlaklığı"nı öldürür
3. **symmetrical mirrored composition** — kompozisyon disiplini
4. **art-nouveau arched frame with keyline border** — poster iskeleti
5. **silkscreen texture, slight misregistration** — baskı hissi
